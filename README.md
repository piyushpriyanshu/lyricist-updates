# Lyricist updates

Release files for the Lyricist Android app (Hindi lyrics). The app reads `update.json` and installs newer versions by itself.

- **Latest:** 3.3.0 ([download APK](apk/Lyricist-3.3.0.apk))
- **Manual install:** open the APK on your phone and allow installs from your browser or file manager.

What's new in 3.3.0:

- Tap the floating lyrics: 4 lines, previous / play / next, and a timing nudge (lyrics earlier or later)
- Floating lyrics colours (or from the song's cover), opacity, snap to the screen edge, hide over chosen apps or games and videos
- Wrong song? Pick the right one — remembered for that track; your line corrections survive lyric updates
- Collections in your library
- Back up and restore saved lyrics (Google Drive or a file)
- Find saved songs from a remembered line, even misspelt
- Share lyric cards straight to Instagram Stories
- Crash and error log in Settings → Diagnostics
